01 — About

Cryptography with foundations—and consequences.

I am Professor of Computer Science and Chair for Cryptography at Ruhr University Bochum, a member of the Horst Görtz Institute for IT Security, and a speaker of the CASA Cluster of Excellence. My work connects rigorous security theory with the protocols and standards used in practice.

04 — Open positions

Join the research group.

We are always interested in hearing from outstanding students and postdoctoral researchers seeking well-funded positions. If you would like to apply for a Ph.D. or postdoctoral position, please contact me by email. Use Mango as your email subject to show that you have read this text.

Your application should include:

  • A clear explanation of why you are interested in joining my research group
  • Your curriculum vitae
  • Complete academic transcripts

Applicants for postdoctoral positions are expected to have published papers at IACR conferences or in relevant journals.

AI-generated mass emails and messages containing tracking pixels will be ignored.
