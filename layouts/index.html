01 — About

Cryptography with foundations—and consequences.

I am Professor of Computer Science and Chair of Cryptography at Ruhr University Bochum, a member of the Horst Görtz Institute for IT Security, and a speaker of the CASA Cluster of Excellence. My work connects rigorous security theory with the protocols and standards used in practice.
